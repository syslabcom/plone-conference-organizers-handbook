## Method

- **sha256** is computed from the file on disk at every run; the manifests carry it per row.
- **Licence**: plone.org pages carry the footer "licensed by the Plone Foundation under a Creative
  Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license" → `cc-by-sa-4` (attachments under
  plone.org get medium confidence because the footer is not inside the PDF). Explicit CC BY on the
  page → `cc-by`. Internet Archive captures → `wayback-copy-of-third-party`. Everything else →
  `all-rights-reserved-or-unknown`.
- **Recommendation**: `keep` by default. `keep-with-redaction-note` = keep, but publish with a note
  (provenance/attribution for Wayback copies; redaction or annotation where personal data is likely).
  `exclude-pending-review` = unknown or reserved licence on a commercial or independent third-party
  site, or likely personal data without an open licence. Unknown-licence pages on
  Plone-community-controlled sites (ploneconf.org subdomains, community.plone.org, the
  Plone-Conference-Devs GitHub wiki) stay `keep` but are flagged for owner confirmation.
- **Personal data**: the brief's pattern `[A-Za-z0-9._%+-]+@[A-Za-z0-9.-]+` is counted raw
  (`email_regex_hits`, dominated by false positives such as `@media`, `@2x.png`, npm versions). Hits
  are then filtered to plausible addresses and split into role addresses (`info@`, `conf@`, list
  addresses) and personal addresses. International phone patterns are counted in visible text.
  Forum and mailing-list pages are at least `possible` because they publish poster identities. Files
  with hits were sample-inspected; resulting overrides are recorded in `audit_rules.OVERRIDES`.
- **Owner inclusion (2026-11-02)**: the publicly available `exclude-pending-review` sources and S089
  are `keep` by owner decision after personal data in the archived file was replaced by
  `[personal data removed — <kind>]` markers (`audit_rules.OWNER_INCLUDED`). Their personal-data
  flag is recomputed from the redacted file; licence status is unchanged; the `notes` column
  records what was removed and what residue stays (role addresses, switchboard numbers).
