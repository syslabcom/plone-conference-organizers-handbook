# Corrections and additions to the 2026 case file — 2 November 2026

Recorded from the organizer's (Alex) review of the draft case file on
2 November 2026. Registered in the handbook as internal source S111. Each item
landed in `docs/conferences/2026-maastricht/conference-2026-maastricht.md` and in
ledger rows L-5069 to L-5076; two earlier rows (L-5043, L-5017) were corrected.

## Corrections of fact

1. Budgeting and accounting were in place earlier than the issue tracker
   suggests. The shared-budget and sponsor-invoicing issues were closed late
   (2026-09-10), but the tooling existed and was used; the closure dates misled
   the retrospective analysis. (Corrects L-5043 and lesson L4.)
2. The platform fee is verified: the conference paid 2.89 EUR per ticket that
   was paid via Stripe. (Corrects L-5017, fact F9.)
3. The speaker ratio is verified against the conference website on 2026-11-02:
   56 listed presenters against 117 tickets, about 48 percent. (Fact F10, row
   L-5075.)
4. F12 (Foundation annual meeting needs more than 25 minutes and should be
   streamed) is confirmed for the 2026 edition. The general rule still awaits
   Foundation confirmation as register row R-034. (Row L-5076.)
5. The grant request was asked repeatedly and never answered; the team did not
   count the money as income. The "did not do" item about counting an
   unanswered grant as income is removed.
6. Recording had an accountable owner; the "did not do" item about leaving
   recording without one is removed. Added to F11 instead: recording equipment
   was purchased and is likely not being reused afterwards. (Row L-5072.)
7. The reference to the critical schedule-search defect is removed from lesson
   L4 in the case file and the collaboration review.

## Additions

8. Many interesting ideas — university outreach, decision-maker research,
   contact to local industry — were started but drowned in a large number of
   surprises that could have been prevented if a manual like this handbook had
   been available. The amount of surprises led to improvisation that made
   planning obsolete. (Row L-5073.)
9. Unexpected tax issues cost a lot of time; the consequences of organizing a
   conference in another country were underestimated. (Row L-5074.)
10. L4 gains: reuse of the conference website tooling can be increased, as can
    reuse of content that recurs every edition. Dependency on external
    deployments is a factor for delays. (Row L-5069.)
11. It is a major source of planning risk that many participants and sponsors
    decide only weeks or days before the conference on their attendance,
    because it also affects the production of material. (Row L-5070.)
12. Knowing whether the budget holds affects a chain of decisions, such as how
    much money is available to cross-finance global south tickets, or what
    extra equipment or keynote speakers can be hired. (Row L-5071.)
13. New "might do differently" item: reach out personally to the known
    participants and ask them for a commitment to come or not come, even if
    they only buy late in the process. (Row L-5070.)
14. Two handover deliverables will not happen for 2026 and are removed from the
    case file's handover table: the asset and credential handover, and the
    vendor contacts. The handbook keeps both in the handover contract for
    future editions.
