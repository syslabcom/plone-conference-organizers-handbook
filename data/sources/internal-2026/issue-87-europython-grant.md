Subject: Grant application – Plone Conference 2026, Maastricht (Global South ticket support)

Dear EuroPython Society Grants Workgroup,

Greetings from Maastricht! We are organising the 2026 Plone Conference in Maastricht, the Netherlands, on behalf of the international Plone community. Plone is one of the oldest and most successful projects built on Python, and the conference is the community's main annual gathering.

Last year the organisers from Finland Plone Conference 2025 also successfully applied for the grant.

We are applying again for a EuroPython Society Grant with one clear, central purpose: to subsidise conference tickets for attendees from the Global South. Travel and registration costs are the single biggest barrier keeping developers, contributors and users from these regions away from international Python and Plone events. By offering substantially discounted tickets to attendees from the Global South, we want to make the 2026 conference genuinely accessible to people who are otherwise priced out, and bring a wider range of voices and backgrounds into the community.

Here is the information requested in your application checklist:

**1) Event name, date(s), location, and website**
Plone Conference 2026
Date: September 21st till 27th 
Location: Maastricht, the Netherlands
Website conference: https://2026.ploneconf.org
Website plone: https://plone.org

**2) Description of the conference / programme**
Expected attendance: [redacted: D7 projected count]
Duration: Training 21st and 22nd, Plone conference 23rd till 25th, Spring 26th and 27th
Target audience: developers, designers, content managers, project managers, open-source contributors and students.
Tracks/talks: 2-3 parallel tracks per day, with a mix of beginner-friendly and non-technical sessions (case studies, community and open source) alongside advanced technical talks, plus [NUMBER] keynotes. A development sprint lets attendees both learn and contribute directly to open source.
Programme/schedule: not yet confirmed but will be posted here https://2026.ploneconf.org/schedule

**3) Amount requested and purpose**
We are requesting [redacted: D7 amount] to fund a Global South ticket subsidy scheme. The grant would allow us to offer 20 heavily reduced or free tickets to attendees from eligible countries, lowering the registration price from [redacted: D7 amount] to [redacted: D7 amount] (as reference https://2026.ploneconf.org/tickets). The entire grant would go directly toward improving access and participation — no part of it would be spent on general event operations. We expect around 20 people from outside Europe to come to the event: from South Africa, from Brasil, from India

**4) Event's overall budget (income, expenses)**
Projected income: [redacted: D7 amount]
Projected expenses: [redacted: D7 amount]
The aim is to come close to break even with additional funding. The missing budget will be funded by the organising companies as contribution to the plone community.

**5) In case the event makes a surplus — what it will be used for**
Any surplus will be reinvested into the Plone community: first toward making the next edition of the conference possible, and toward the non-profit Plone Foundation to support future community events. None of it would be distributed privately.

**6) Background on the organising team**
The event is organised by Cosent BV and Syslab.com. The core team is 4 people, supported by 6 volunteers and 0 paid staff. Collectively the team has been involved in organising many plone conferences dating back to 2016, so we know what to expect and how to run an event of this scale.
Code of conduct: https://2026.ploneconf.org/about/code-of-conduct and https://plone.org/foundation/about/materials/foundation-resolutions/code-of-conduct

**7) How EPS funding will be acknowledged**
We will display the EPS logo prominently on the conference website (front page and sponsors section) and acknowledge EPS support in the opening sponsor slide shown before each recorded talk (all talks will be recorded and published after the event). We will mention EPS support across our social media channels before, during and after the conference, and we would be glad to host a EuroPython table or booth — and to add your stickers to the attendee swag bags. We will of course promote the EuroPython conference within our community, since it funds this programme.

After the event we will send you a short write-up covering how it went and the impact of the grant — including how many Global South attendees the subsidy reached — with photos you are welcome to use on the EPS blog.

Thank you very much for considering our application. Please let me know if you need any further information.

We are requesting [redacted: D7 amount] to fund a Global South ticket subsidy scheme. Unit economics:
- Regular ticket price: [redacted: D7 amount] (see https://2026.ploneconf.org/tickets)
- Subsidised Global South ticket: [redacted: D7 amount]
- Subsidy per recipient: [redacted: D7 amount]
- Number of beneficiaries: 20
- Total: [redacted: D7 amount]

We have set out the economics explicitly so the impact of any change to the grant is immediately visible: every [redacted: D7 amount] funds one additional Global South attendee. Grants that help other people participate are EPS's stated top priority, and this programme is a direct application of that mission.

We expect around 20–30 people from the Global South to come to the event, which depends largely on the availability of reduced tickets: especially from South Africa, from Brazil, and from India.

Eligibility. To qualify for a subsidised ticket, an applicant must:
- be resident in a country on the World Bank low- or lower-middle-income economies list (https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/906519);
- not be employer-sponsored for full-price attendance (self-declared);
- commit to attending at least three conference days.

Prioritisation if we receive more applications than tickets (20 in total). We will score applicants as follows:
- 40 pts — Community engagement (GitHub, meetups, blog, prior open-source work)
- 30 pts — Intent (what they plan to do at the conference and what they will bring back)
- 20 pts — Self-declared financial need
- 10 pts — Cohort balance adjustment by organisers (country, diversity, first-timers, gender, etc.)

Fair scoring. Scoring is carried out independently by two scorers (one person from the organisation team, one advisor from the Plone community); any disagreement is escalated to the Plone Foundation Board. Anonymised results will be published in the post-event impact report.
